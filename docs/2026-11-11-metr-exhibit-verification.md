## Second pass, same day

Both posts load on x.com in an ordinary browser without signing in (automated
fetches are what X blocks), so they were read there and the wording confirmed. The
mirror links (teapawt.org, nitter.cf) and the `limits` sentence about them are
removed: the exhibit now links only to the posts.

- **Ladish excerpt restored in full.** The first pass cut the two middle sentences:
  "I pray and hope there will be strongly diminishing returns before long in this
  process. But leaders at METR and Anthropic both seem to think there might not be."
  They are what "That’s fucking terrifying" refers to. With them elided the remark
  read as a reaction to the graph alone, and his hope for diminishing returns was lost.
- **METR's own caveats restored**, each found in the report text: "would not be
  surprised" by 1.3x or 3x; "most METR staff who worked on this project"; robustness
  analyses "between 1.1x and 2.9x"; Anthropic "could unilaterally approve or reject
  our requests"; METR "did not request or receive access to sample from non-public
  models"; "significant uncertainty and internal disagreements". "Ruled out" faster
  compute growth is now "is confident", which is METR's word. "Earlier catch-up" is
  now what METR wrote: a lower historical growth rate than the industry frontier.
- **Cotra** is described as "of METR, writing in a personal capacity", and her
  extinction sentence follows her wording more closely ("would likely disempower
  humanity, and this could lead to human extinction").
- **Sherburn's affiliation** follows his site: "Research Engineer (Contract),
  Preparedness Team, OpenAI", and "Co-founder, P-Zero Research". The site lists the
  OpenAI role in the past tense but does not give an end date. Ladish's affiliation
  (Palisade Research) is taken from his X profile.
- The `comparison` field is renamed `ai2027`, since both renderers label it
  "The AI 2027 comparison".

Not changed, and a judgement call: the title leads with "warnings of superintelligence
