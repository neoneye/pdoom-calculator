Slider mockups

These are working mockups, so drag them. Each one is the P(powerful AI) card redesigned.

A

Give the curve the same knob

The curve gets the same ring knob as the ± slider, sitting under its peak. Both controls now look movable, and you can still drag the curve itself.

Smallest change. The card still has two separate controls.

B

Two ordinary sliders

The curve is only a picture. Underneath are two identical sliders, labelled "Your best guess" and "How sure are you". Everybody knows how to use a slider.

Most familiar. The curve stops being something you touch, so the card loses some of its character.

C

Place it yourself

There's no curve until you choose one. The quiz's suggestion shows as a dashed outline, and you either tap the scale or keep the suggestion. Nobody can submit without deciding.

Fixes the "left it where the quiz put it" problem directly. It adds one click per card, three in total.

D

One control: knob plus edge grips

The ± slider goes away. A knob moves the curve, and two grips on the dashed lines make the range wider or narrower, so the curve itself is the whole control.

Tidiest. The grips are small targets on phones, and the meaning of the grips is less obvious than a labelled slider.
